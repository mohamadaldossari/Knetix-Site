Questions

Asked before every deal

What is a performance-based marketing agency?+
An agency that earns its fee from the results it generates instead of a fixed monthly retainer. KNETIX charges 10% of the new revenue our ads produce, verified through Triple Whale attribution. If the ads don't sell, we don't get paid.
How does the 10% revenue share work?+
We install Triple Whale on your store so every sale is attributed to its source. Each billing cycle, our fee is 10% of revenue attributed to campaigns we run — calculated from data you can verify yourself in your own dashboard.
Why the $3,000/month ad spend minimum?+
Below roughly $3K/month there isn't enough data to test creative properly, and not enough revenue for a 10% share to cover our work. The minimum protects both sides of the deal.
Do I have to sign a contract?+
No. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees. You can walk away at any time — we keep clients by performing, not by locking them in.
What's included in the free audit?+
The 4-Angle Audit reviews your creative, copy, offer and funnel, identifies the biggest leaks in your account, and delivers a recorded walkthrough within 48 hours — free, whether or not you hire us.
How do you prove which sales came from your ads?+
Triple Whale — third-party attribution that bypasses iOS14 limitations. You get your own dashboard login, so any revenue claim we make can be verified independently in about 30 seconds.
