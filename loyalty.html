Asked before every build.

Do my customers need an app?+

No. They scan a QR code and sign up in the browser in about 30 seconds. Nothing to install, for them or for you.

How is this different from a punch card?+

A punch card gets lost and tells you nothing. This lives on the customer's phone, tracks every visit automatically, and gives you a searchable list of every customer with their number.

Will it spam my customers?+

No. Customers opt in themselves, every promo text has one-tap opt-out, and the system cannot text anyone who has opted out. Reward texts only go out when someone actually earns one.

What if I want to cancel?+

Month-to-month, 30 days' notice, no penalty.

How long until it is live?+

Within 24 hours of you sending your details. We build and brand the whole thing for you, then hand you your login and QR code.
