The things everyone asks first.

Is it really commission-only?+
Yes. There's no base and no cost to join. You earn 50% of the one-time setup fee on every business you sign, a standard close is around $225 to you, a premium one around $375. The recurring monthly fee stays with the company; your money is the setup fee.
Do I need sales experience?+
No. The certification takes you from zero, what the product is, who to target, how to reach out, how to handle objections, and how to close. Once you pass, the cockpit hands you scripts and an AI drafter so you're never staring at a blank message.
How much can I actually make?+
It's uncapped and depends on how much you close. Standard shops pay you around $225 per signing, premium shops around $375. Three premium closes is over $1,000. There's no limit on how many you can sign.
Is it remote? Do I need to be in a certain city?+
It's fully remote. You can DM shops from anywhere, or walk into local businesses in your own city, whatever converts. You work on your own schedule from your phone.
How do I get started?+
Join the Discord, head to the become-a-rep channel, and start the free certification. When you pass the final assessment, we set up your cockpit account and unlock the rep zone. From there you claim your first lead and start closing.
When and how do I get paid?+
Your commission locks in the moment the client signs and pays their setup fee. The rate at signing is yours on that deal for good. Payout is handled directly, you'll see the full breakdown on your commission tile.
