What we actually do

Most local businesses run on repeat customers, and most of them have no system for keeping those customers. The regulars are tracked in the owner's head. The person who came in once and never returned is invisible. Punch cards get lost in the wash.

We fix that with a loyalty and referral system built specifically for the shop, branded in their colours, with their logo and their name on it. Customers scan a QR code at the counter, sign up in about thirty seconds, and every visit gets tracked from then on. No app to download, nothing for the customer to remember.

On the owner's side it's a private dashboard: every customer, how often they come in, who's earned a reward, who referred whom. Text messaging and online booking are built in. The daily job is one tap.

The part owners don't expect is the customer list. They sign up for a digital punch card and end up with a record of their entire client base, which most local shops have never had, and which turns out to be worth more than the rewards.

We work mostly with barbershops, nail salons, car detailing shops, vape stores, lash and brow studios, and pet groomers, businesses where customers come back on a cycle. If your customers buy once and never return, we're honestly not the right fit, and we'll tell you that.

How we work

  • Done for you. You don't configure anything. Send your colours, logo, and what you want the reward to be, and it's built and live, usually within a day.
  • Month to month. Thirty days' notice, no long contracts, no cancellation penalty. If it's not working for your shop, you leave.
  • Your data stays yours. We don't sell customer data, and if you cancel you can export your customer list and keep it.
  • Straight answers. If a loyalty program isn't right for your business, we'd rather say so than sell you one that sits unused.
